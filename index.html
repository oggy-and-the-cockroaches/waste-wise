Slide 3 & 4: Problem Statement

The Dual Dilemma of Modern Waste Systems

Municipal waste management currently fails on two distinct ends: citizens mix recyclables at the household level, while city sanitation trucks drive blindly along fixed, inefficient routes.

Sorting Dilemma

Improper Waste Segregation

Households and commercial units fail to separate recyclable polymers, metals, and organics. Once dry recyclable paper or plastic gets contaminated by wet food waste, recyclability drops to near zero.

  • Recyclable plastics, glass, and compostable wet waste get mixed into a single black bag.
  • Citizens find confusing municipal color codes and resin classifications hard to follow.
  • Landfills overflow with recoverable materials, generating methane and leachate runoff.
Collection Dilemma

Inefficient Collection Routes

Sanitation fleets follow static pre-printed schedules that traverse the entire district daily without knowing bin fill status, causing heavy trucks to visit empty bins while overflowing bins sit untouched.

  • Wasteful criss-cross transit burns excessive taxpayer diesel fuel and creates congestion.
  • Severe tailpipe greenhouse gas emissions ($CO_2$, particulate matter) from municipal trucks.
  • Overflowing street bins remain unserviced for hours, causing public health hazards.

Slide 8 & 9: Architecture & Tech Stack

Engineered System Architecture

WasteWise connects citizen-facing web clients with an asynchronous API gateway, neural vision classification, and spatial PostgreSQL/PostGIS routing engines.

01 / FRONTEND
Next.js Web Client

Citizen camera capture, driver navigation dashboard, and responsive municipal operations portal.

02 / BACKEND
FastAPI Service

High-throughput async Python backend handling image inference dispatches and spatial geospatial queries.

03 / AI VISION
Image Classifier

Convolutional neural model trained on multi-class municipal solid waste datasets (plastic, paper, metal, organic).

04 / SPATIAL
PostGIS & TSP Solver

PostgreSQL geospatial engine running Traveling Salesperson heuristics for optimal fuel-efficient paths.

Slide 6: Scope & Roadmap

First Version MVP Scope

As outlined in Slide 6 of the Skill Lab project proposal, the first working version demonstrates the core principles of AI sorting and route optimization.

Upload Waste Images

Simple drag-and-drop or mobile camera image ingestion interface that preprocesses images for neural classification.

Identify Waste Categories

Detects core municipal solid waste classes: plastic polymers, recyclable paper/cardboard, metals, and compostable organic matter.

Disposal Recommendations

Clear color-coded bin recommendations (Green for wet, Blue for dry recyclables, Red for hazardous) and preparation guidelines.

Collection Route Prototype

Heuristic TSP engine that demonstrates quantifiable reductions in transit kilometers and fuel burn across municipal collection points.
